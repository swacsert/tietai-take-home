# 1c · Pre-kickoff questions — prioritized

Ten questions for San Andrés before writing a line of code. Order: first what changes **scope**, then what has **long external lead times**, then what changes the **design**, and last what defines **how we prove it works**. Each one says what changes in the plan depending on the answer.

| # | Question | Asked to | What changes in the plan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What research question must the week-12 cohort answer, which service do those patients come from (oncology, cardiology, diabetes), how many years of history does it need, and who signs it off as valid? | Research lead | Oncology → HIS + FHIR first, openEHR analysed but not loaded. Diabetes/cardiology → the openEHR channel moves before week 6 and Measurement goes up in priority. Years of history decide whether new messages are enough or a one-off historical extract is needed. No sign-off owner → no definition of "done". |
| 2 | We propose to pseudonymise inside the hospital, before anything leaves, with a key held by the hospital. Does the DPO approve pseudonymised data reaching a multi-tenant platform in GCP europe-west1, or must nothing leave? Is there already an ethics committee (CEIm) approval for this secondary use? | Hospital management + DPO | Approved → current architecture. Not approved → identity resolution and mapping must run inside the hospital (a bigger edge host from Marta). No ethics approval → week 12 delivers a ready pipeline, not a cohort. |
| 3 | Marta, what is the real lead time from request to a machine for Hydra Connect on the clinical network (VM or physical server) with read access to the HIS drop folder and one outbound rule to port 443? When are your change windows, is there a vendor security review first, and where should the hospital-owned OMOP database live and who will administer it? | Marta | 2 weeks → current plan. 4–6 weeks → the request goes out on day 1 and week 2 becomes profiling of copied files inside the hospital. OMOP inside the hospital → a second network path and a second request to Marta. |
| 4 | Which patient identifier does each system use (HIS, oncology FHIR, chronic-care openEHR)? Is it the same medical record number, or is there an EMPI? How are duplicate-patient merges handled — do they arrive as messages (ADT^A40)? | Marta + Dr. Ruiz | Shared number or EMPI → a cross-reference table. Separate identifiers and no EMPI → demographic matching and a large review queue. Merges not propagated → duplicated people in the cohort. |
| 5 | How many protected hours per week does Dr. Ruiz have for this project, in which slots, and who covers for him on call or on holiday? Is anyone else able to review mappings? | Hospital management + Dr. Ruiz | His sign-off gates weeks 2, 8 and 11. Two hours a week → fixed sessions reviewing only doubtful and most frequent codes. No real hours → escalate before kickoff. |
| 6 | What share of Diego's working time is allocated to this project over the 12 weeks, and do on-call duty or other tasks take him away? | Hospital management + Marta | ~50% → he runs DLQ replay with us from week 6. ~10% → much simpler runbook and alerts, and a support period from us after week 12. |
| 7 | How does the HIS write the drop files — one message or batches, direct write or temp name and rename, how often? Can it resend the same message? Who deletes files and after how many days? Can we have one real day of files before kickoff? | Marta (and the HIS vendor) | No atomic rename → read only once the file is stable. Resends → deduplication from day one. Deleted after 24 h → a weekend outage loses data with no ACK to notice. A real day lets us profile before the edge host exists. |
| 8 | Which CIE-10-ES edition does the HIS (and oncology) code with, has it changed over the period we will load, are there local codes, and are diagnoses coded by clinical documentation staff or typed as free text? | Dr. Ruiz | Several editions → mapping versioned by date. Local codes → review queue and more of Dr. Ruiz's time. Free text → diagnoses without codes and a smaller cohort. |
| 9 | Are the HIS Z-segments documented? Does any of them carry data the cohort needs (main diagnosis, service), and which feeds do you already know are non-conformant? | Dr. Ruiz + Marta | A needed field only in a Z-segment → it must be parsed and mapped. No documentation → the week-2 profiling report becomes that documentation, reviewed with Dr. Ruiz. |
| 10 | In the last integration that went wrong, what exactly failed and how did you find out? What would make you trust the data this time? | Dr. Ruiz + Marta | Turns their past experience into acceptance criteria: e.g. silently lost data → a reconciliation report (received vs loaded) at every delivery; changes without notice → nothing reaches production without their validation in pre-production. |
